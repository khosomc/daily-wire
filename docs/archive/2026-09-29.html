Pakistan pressed its own account of the Afghan war on a visiting US commission and hosted India at its first SCO planning meeting, even as Kabul accused Islamabad over deadly clashes in Nuristan. Geopolitics & Diplomacy

Munir presses Pakistan's view on US Afghanistan War Commission

Field Marshal Asim Munir asked the visiting commission to factor in the regional view and Pakistan's experience when assessing America's 20-year war. According to ISPR, the delegation acknowledged Pakistan's role and sacrifices; Arab News notes the commission's earlier findings also criticised Pakistan for giving the Taliban sanctuary.

Source: Dawn · Arab News Pakistan · Express Tribune

India attends Pakistan-chaired SCO coordinators' meeting in Islamabad

An Indian delegation took part despite frozen bilateral ties as Pakistan opened its 2026–27 SCO chairmanship programme. The talks are laying the groundwork for a heads-of-state summit in Pakistan in August 2027.

Source: Express Tribune · Independent Urdu (Urdu)

Pakistan seeks joint funding push for trans-Afghan railway

Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal proposed that Pakistan and Uzbekistan jointly seek international financing for the line through Afghanistan, which is still at feasibility stage. He also sought support for the $2.5bn upgrade of the Karachi–Rohri section of ML-1, where the ADB is now leading financing after stalled talks with China.

Source: Arab News Pakistan

Conflict & Security

Islamabad rejects Kabul's claims over deadly Nuristan clashes

Afghanistan's Taliban government said it killed 28 fighters who had crossed from Pakistan and alleged they were recruited by Pakistani intelligence. Pakistan's information ministry called the claims baseless and urged Kabul to act against militants on its own soil.

Source: Al Jazeera · The News

Centre raises governor's rule threat as KP stays defiant

Federal ministers said the legal grounds for governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa were met and accused the PTI government of failing against militancy. Chief Minister Sohail Afridi refused to call off the party's 4 October march on Islamabad, and Governor Faisal Karim Kundi urged him to drop it on security grounds.

Source: Dawn · Arab News Pakistan · Khyber News

Security forces report 10 militants killed in KP and Balochistan

State media said four militants it linked to the banned TTP were killed in an intelligence-based operation in Dera Ismail Khan, after six were reported killed in separate KP and Balochistan operations earlier on Monday.

Source: Dawn · Express Tribune

Mediators to meet Iran and US as Hormuz stays closed

Qatari mediators were expected to meet Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in New York and the US side separately, a Reuters report in Business Recorder said. Araghchi said any reopening of the Strait of Hormuz depends on Iran's terms being met, BBC Urdu reported, while President Trump said the US would win the war "very soon".
