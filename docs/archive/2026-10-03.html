Dar says more than six countries want to join Makkah pact

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar told a press conference in Islamabad that at least six Muslim countries had shown strong interest in joining the defence pact Pakistan shares with Saudi Arabia and Türkiye. He did not name them in the reports.

Conflict & Security

Two civilians killed by Indian border fire near Kasur

Pakistani security sources said India's Border Security Force shot dead two unarmed villagers from Ladhar and wounded a third after they went slightly beyond a border pillar, state radio reported via Dawn. Arab News said the two sides gave conflicting accounts of what happened.

Source: Dawn · Arab News Pakistan

Violence-linked deaths up 83% in third quarter, CRSS finds

The Centre for Research and Security Studies counted 1,415 deaths and 558 injuries in 385 incidents in July–September 2026, with Balochistan accounting for nearly 59% of fatalities. It named the TTP as the deadliest group, Dawn reported.

Source: Dawn

Seven militants killed in North Waziristan; policeman killed in Tank

Security forces killed seven militants in an intelligence-based operation in North Waziristan's Eidak area, state media said, citing security sources. Separately, attackers with guns and a grenade killed a policeman guarding a hospital in Jandola, Tank district.

Source: Dawn · Dawn

Freed tanker crew due home as families of dead get compensation

Four of the ten Pakistani crew of the hijacked tanker MT Honour 25 were due in Karachi on Saturday, Ishaq Dar said. The prime minister's office announced Rs12.5m and a house for each family of the three sailors killed during the vessel's recovery off Somalia.

Source: Dawn · Arab News Pakistan

Dar said the Strategic Political Defence Committee set up under the pact between Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye will meet in Riyadh next week to review the surge in Houthi attacks on the kingdom.

Corps commanders back Saudi pact and warn against 'May 9-like' unrest

The 277th Corps Commanders' Conference, chaired by Field Marshal Asim Munir, endorsed the trilateral pact, condemned Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and restated Pakistan's commitment to the security of the holy mosques. ISPR said any attempt to stir a 'May 9-like situation' at home would be met with decisive force.
