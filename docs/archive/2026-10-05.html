Geopolitics & Diplomacy

Iran links Hormuz reopening to Islamabad interim deal conditions

Iran says the Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until seven conditions in its June interim agreement with the US, known as the Islamabad MoU, are met, according to a Reuters report citing Iran's Nournews carried by Arab News. The statement came days after Tehran received Washington's reply to its latest diplomatic proposal, keeping Pakistan's mediation central to the dispute.

KP emergency back on table after talks collapse over counterterrorism

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi said talks with the opposition had collapsed and accused KP Chief Minister Sohail Afridi of sabotaging them, while the PTI blamed the Centre's insistence on 'unilateral' counterterrorism action in the province. KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi, after meeting Naqvi, said he was ready to play his constitutional role if an emergency were deemed necessary.

The leader of Yemen's Saudi-backed government announced a major military operation to seize all areas under Houthi control, with government forces reporting strikes in Sanaa and Saada, even as the Houthis made fresh ground gains around a government-held city. The escalation adds pressure on Pakistan's commitments under its defence pact with Riyadh.

Makkah pact committee meets in Riyadh today

A committee of the Makkah defence pact between Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye meets in Riyadh on Monday, the Turkish foreign ministry said, with Turkish ministers including Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan attending. Ankara said the talks would cover recent regional developments and progress in giving the alliance an institutional form.
