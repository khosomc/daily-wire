Geopolitics & Diplomacy

UN chief arrives for three-day visit centred on US–Iran mediation

António Guterres visits Pakistan from 6 to 8 October at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, with talks expected to cover Islamabad's mediation between Washington and Tehran and a stop at the UN military observer mission (UNMOGIP). The Foreign Office said the trip, in the final months of Guterres's term, would highlight Pakistan's support for multilateralism.

Iran calls US talks meaningless as Washington targets foreign banks

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian said negotiating with the US had only been followed by fresh American attacks, state media reported, while the US warned foreign financial institutions they could face sanctions for dealing with Iran's banking sector. The hardening on both sides complicates the Islamabad-brokered track that Guterres is visiting to support.

Source: Arab News Pakistan · Arab News Pakistan · Dawn

At Moscow talks, Pakistan defends strikes on Afghan militant bases

Special Representative Mohammad Sadiq told the Moscow format meeting that terrorism lay at the core of Afghanistan's problems and that Pakistan would keep taking 'all appropriate measures' against sanctuaries there. Taliban Foreign Minister Amir Khan Muttaqi said at the same gathering that the two neighbours' disputes could be settled through diplomacy, and Independent Urdu reported that Russia offered to help ease tensions.

Defence

Makkah pact partners agree rapid force deployment in Saudi Arabia

After an emergency meeting in Riyadh attended by Field Marshal Asim Munir and Ishaq Dar, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye agreed to provide designated forces and capabilities for rapid deployment in the kingdom in response to Houthi attacks, the Foreign Office said. Pakistan's Lieutenant General Nauman Mahmood was named the alliance's first secretary-general for three years, and US Secretary of State Marco Rubio separately said Washington would honour its own security commitments to Riyadh.
