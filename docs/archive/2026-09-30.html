Tarar tells Taliban to abandon 'militant mindset'

Information Minister Attaullah Tarar said Pakistan wants peaceful coexistence with Afghanistan but described the Taliban government's approach as the root of the two countries' problems. He urged Kabul to act as a responsible state amid continuing attacks Islamabad links to Afghan soil.

New US Iran sanctions round names parties in Pakistan

Washington sanctioned ten individuals and firms in China, Pakistan, Turkiye, Iran and Saudi Arabia over alleged support for Iran's military, Al Jazeera reported. The move keeps pressure on Pakistani businesses with Iranian links as Islamabad weighs its exposure to US sanctions.

Pakistan pitches its ports as Central Asia's sea outlet

At a CAREC conference, Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal urged regional governments and lenders to upgrade roads, border crossings and rail freight so landlocked Central Asian states can reach Arabian Sea ports. He called for turning CAREC routes into full economic corridors covering logistics, energy and digital links.

Conflict & Security

Three Pakistani sailors killed before Somali forces free tanker

Puntland maritime police said five crew members of the MT Honour 25, including three Pakistanis, were killed by pirates before its forces retook the tanker seized in April; about ten Pakistanis were on board. Islamabad said it was working to confirm the reports, and the Express Tribune said 16 suspected pirates were detained.
