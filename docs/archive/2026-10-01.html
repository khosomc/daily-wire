Indian SCO coordinator in Islamabad as Dar opens chairmanship talks

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif said Pakistan would use whatever means it has to defend Saudi Arabia from the Houthis or any other threat, calling Riyadh's enemies Pakistan's enemies. It is among Islamabad's clearest signals yet that it could be drawn directly into the regional conflict.

Officials told the IMF mission the war's economic impact had passed, projecting 7.5% inflation if Brent holds near $80 and a rupee stable around 278 to the dollar, the Express Tribune reported. The Fund urged a market-based exchange rate and tighter monetary policy.

Private firms may get to import LNG directly

The Petroleum Division is weighing wider auctions of unused LNG terminal capacity so power plants and private buyers can import gas themselves, after two terminals sat largely idle since Qatari supplies were disrupted in March. The plan, first reported by Bloomberg, would loosen state-owned Pakistan LNG's hold on spot purchases.

Finance & Capital

Public debt reaches Rs86.7tr, up 76% in four years

Finance ministry figures show public debt rose from Rs49.3tr in June 2022 to Rs86.7tr by June 2026, of which Rs27.3tr is external. The government plans Rs6.86tr in fresh borrowing in FY27 and aims to shift from short-term bills to longer bonds and sukuk.

Source: Dawn

Retail investors to trade government debt on the stock exchange

Under an IMF-linked plan to deepen the local-currency bond market, the government said the public will be able to buy and sell treasury bills and bonds through the stock market. The move aims to broaden the investor base beyond banks.

Source: Dawn · Arab News Pakistan

KSE-100 gives back most of early IMF-driven rally

The benchmark index touched 171,493 on hopes for the IMF talks and the bond-market plan, but late selling left it just 369 points higher at 169,969. Analysts cited crude prices near $107 a barrel and the strain on the import bill.
