Pakistan carried its fight with Afghanistan-based militants across the border again, with air strikes in Kunar and Helmand that Islamabad says killed 22 fighters and Kabul says killed civilians, while the military spokesman vowed to do 'everything necessary' and told India its Kashmir 'encounter' claim was staged. Abroad, Washington's sanctions on a Pakistani defence executive over alleged Iran procurement tested Islamabad's balancing act, and the army called its commitment to Saudi security 'absolute'. On money, inflation eased to 10.3% and the finance minister set a B+ rating target for 2027, but the KSE-100 suffered a sharp sell-off on oil and US–Iran uncertainty.

US sanctions Pakistani defence executive over alleged Iran supply network

BBC Urdu reported that Washington has sanctioned Waseem Pasha Tajammul and three companies of his Cavalier Group, alleging they supported Iran's defence ministry. Separately, the Foreign Office told its weekly briefing that Pakistan observes all 'applicable' sanctions on trade with Iran under international and domestic law, Independent Urdu reported.
