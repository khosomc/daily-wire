Military business directory Pilot

Who owns what in Pakistan's military-linked corporate sector, documented from company filings, stock-exchange data and credit-rating reports. This pilot covers companies listed, or recently listed, on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, where ownership is a matter of public record.

Military welfare foundations
Fauji Foundation (army-linked) and the Army Welfare Trust are registered as charitable, non-profit bodies that invest to fund the welfare of ex-servicemen and their families. Each is run by a committee of administration; their companies describe control as remaining with these committees.
Military-linked
In this directory, a company is military-linked if a military welfare foundation, or a company it controls, is its largest shareholder or has management rights. It does not imply that the armed forces as an institution run the company day to day.
Confirmed
Backed by a primary or near-primary document: stock-exchange data, a company notice or report, or an independent credit-rating report. Linked.
Reported
From credible news or research reporting of filings we could not read directly. Treated as provisional.

What the records show

  • One retired general, Lt Gen (retd) Anwar Ali Hyder — managing director and CEO of Fauji Foundation since April 2024 — chairs the boards of FFC, Fauji Cement, Mari Energies, Askari Bank and Fauji Foods.
  • Stakes are increasingly consolidated: FFBL was folded into FFC in 2024, FFBL Power is being moved fully under FFC, and in 2026 the Army Welfare Trust began transferring its insurance companies to Fauji Foundation.
  • Exchange records lag behind: PSX still lists FFBL as Fauji Foods' parent and the Army Welfare Trust as Askari General's parent after both changed.
